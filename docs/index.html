about me

hi! i'm known online as aloelucidity. i'm a game developer born in 2006 who adores 2000s web culture and making video games.
i'm a jack of all trades... as you can probably tell. i like game design, programming, music composition, spriting... the list goes on :P

those skills are mainly used to create awesome standalone games for you folks to play! i'm also part of the fangame team Solarshine Studios :)

support me!

of course, it's much easier to do it with company, a big part of why i create is to interact with people who enjoy my work.
